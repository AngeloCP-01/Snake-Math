Design System · v1.0

Snake & Math

The visual language for the Snake & Math Android game: a glossy jungle toy box with chunky rounded type, lime for the primary action, gold for numbers and cream for reading. Colors were sampled from the v3 mockups. Sizes are in dp/sp for a 390dp phone. The written spec lives in README.md and the tokens in tokens.json.

Principles

Glossy jungle toy box

Deep forest backgrounds. Only lime, gold and red are loud. If everything glows, nothing does.

Chunky & touchable

Pills, rounded panels, thick outlined type, a 3D lip on the primary action. Touch targets are at least 48dp.

Cream reads, gold counts

All text is cream. Every score is gold. Lime marks the accent word or the main action.

The board is sacred

Decoration stays at the edges and off playable cells. Swipe only, with no arrow buttons.

Math is the hero

On MDAS screens the expression is the biggest text after the logo.

ColorSampled from the mockups. Use semantic tokens in code (surface.panel, text.number…), not raw hex.

Question card

5 + 3 × 2 = ?

Full width minus the 16dp gutters, radius 24, surface.card fill, 2dp border, at least 96dp tall. title.s label with leaf sprigs overlapping the top edge, then the math.expression line. Use real × ÷ glyphs, never x or /.

Board, snake & applesBuilt on Compose Canvas. The game logic is grid-based; the drawing is smooth.

IN PROGRESSThe gameplay art is still being designed in docs/assets/game/ (see snake-assembly-prototype.html and gameplay-snake-v1.md). Snake proportions and colors below are provisional. The preview loads the live asset files, so it updates whenever they change.

Frame: 6dp, leaf-300 → leaf-400 → foliage-500, radius 24 outside and 18 inside, with curls of frame at the corners.

Field: #01411E, 1dp grid in #0D5426. The target cell is 44dp, giving 8 × 9 on a phone. Compute columns as floor(availableWidth / 44).

Decorations: grass tufts and small rocks, drawn under game pieces, never on a cell with the snake or an apple.

Answer apple: 1.15 × cell. Number at 0.5 × cell in cream with a dark red outline. All three apples look identical. The apple art in this preview is a placeholder until the apple asset exists.

Snake rendering · hybrid (provisional)

1 · HeadTransparent PNG snake_head_right_v1.png (1254², faces right). Drawn on top. Fit its visible bounds to the head footprint rather than scaling the whole square. Neck anchor ≈ (195, 675) in source pixels. Rotated about the head cell center for other directions; directional variants are a later art decision.
2 · BodyOne continuous rounded Canvas path through cell centers, never stamped tiles. Layers: drop shadow #001D14 50% → outline #174C03 → vertical fill #BEEF00 → #A5DC00 → #78B700 → highlight #DCFF61 33% offset up-left. Corner radius ≈ 0.5 × cell.
3 · SpotsOval spots alternating #448E06 / #65A700, alternating sides of the centerline, kept well inside the body width.
4 · TailTapered point at the end of the same shape: outline #174C03, fill #9DD400, highlight stroke #D8F75A. No tail image.

Collision stays grid-based. The tongue and head art never enlarge the hit area. Final body width, head scale and neck join are being calibrated in the assembled prototype, so check readability at real board scale (cell ≈ 44dp).

Do / Don't

DOKeep one lime primary action per screen.
DON'TAdd arrow buttons or a D-pad. Controls are swipe only.
DOShow every score in gold number styles.
DON'THighlight the correct answer apple in any way.
DOKeep foliage at screen edges, behind content.
DON'TPut decorations on cells that hold the snake or an apple.
DOUse real × ÷ − glyphs in math.
DON'TIntroduce new hues. Stay within forest, lime, gold, cream and red.
