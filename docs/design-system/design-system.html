Snake & Math

The visual language for the Snake & Math Android game: a glossy jungle toy box with chunky rounded type, lime for the primary action, gold for numbers and cream for reading. Principles

Glossy jungle toy box

Deep forest backgrounds. Only lime, gold and red are loud. If everything glows, nothing does.

Chunky & touchable

Pills, rounded panels, thick outlined type, a 3D lip on the primary action. Touch targets are at least 48dp.

Cream reads, gold counts

All text is cream. Every score is gold. Lime marks the accent word or the main action.

The board is sacred

Decoration stays at the edges and off playable cells. Swipe only, with no arrow buttons.

Math is the hero

On MDAS screens the expression is the biggest text after the logo.

ColorSampled from the mockups. Use semantic tokens in code (surface.panel, text.number…), not raw hex.

Snake rendering · smooth joins v3

1 · HeadThe head region of the atlas path-snake/snake_path_parts_v1.png (2172 × 724): source (30, 155, 653, 445), visible bounds (32, 159, 647, 436). It's top-down and faces right. Drop alpha below 70 at load. Feather the rear 42% with a transparency gradient; the eyes, snout and tongue stay opaque. Height 1.1 × body width, anchored at 59% of its length, and drawn last.
2 · BodyOne swept silhouette along the centerline, 0.70 × cell wide, with no outline. Base #8ABA16. Cylindrical shading comes from 28 strips across the width, from #739D0D at the edges to about #A9D91F in the middle, with the light biased toward one side. Turns are quadratic with radius min(0.36 × cell, 45% of each leg).
3 · SpotsSparse ovals clipped to the body: radius ≈ 0.18 × width (1.24 : 0.85), spaced 0.9 × width, in lanes ±0.24 × width, with a slight wobble. They alternate #72A71A, with every third #649A16. They're anchored to the head end, so they travel with the snake.
4 · Tail & shadowThe tail is the same silhouette tapering over 1.5 × width (radius ∝ √(1−(1−t)²)) to a soft round tip, with no tail image. The shadow is a single stroke #071611 at 40%, 0.8 × width, offset 4px down.

Motion: logic moves one cell per 240ms tick. The renderer interpolates the head along the cardinal segment, the tail follows its next cell (except on growing ticks), and the head rotation eases over the first 35% of a tick. Curves are visual only, and collision stays on the grid.

Answer apple

Procedural (no image), drawn inside its cell. Body #D83D26, highlight #F17D54, stem and leaf #749B36, soft shadow #092019 at 40%. The number is centered at 0.38 × cell, bold, in #FFF4D3 with a 2.5px #9A271E outline. When the font is bundled, use Baloo 2 ExtraBold to match the system. All three apples look identical; the correct one is never highlighted.

Art assets

The atlas supplies the gameplay head only; the body and tail use continuous procedural geometry. Superseded artwork is archived outside the active asset folders. Copy only the three runtime sources in the approved asset allowlist to res/drawable-nodpi and load the atlas once as an ImageBitmap; DrawScope.drawImage(srcOffset, srcSize, dstOffset, dstSize) draws a region. These are review masters, not a rights clearance.

Historical browser rules below: the native target wraps at every edge, Classic ends only on self-hit, and MDAS passes through its body without penalty/reset; only wrong answers cost lives. Native arithmetic uses selectable Easy/Medium/Hard profiles with numeric bounds still awaiting agreement. Read the PRD for current rules; this browser study has not been ported to native gameplay.

Gameplay rulesThe behavior the UI has to communicate, as implemented in the prototype. These are PRD defaults, not final release rules.

InputSwipe or drag only, with a 16px threshold and the dominant axis winning. Up to 2 turns are queued, and a reversal is rejected against the last queued direction. Turns apply at cell boundaries. The first swipe starts the round.
ClassicOne apple. +10 and grow per apple. A wall or self collision means game over. Filling the board means "Garden complete!".
MDASFive lives and three distinct numbered apples. A correct answer gives +10 and grows the snake. A wrong answer costs a life with no growth and moves to the next question. A collision costs a life and resets the snake and question, which then waits for a swipe. No timer.
QuestionsThree operands, standard precedence (× ÷ before + −, left to right), integer results only. Forms: a + b × c, a × b − d, ab ÷ b + c, ab ÷ b × c. Wrong answers are within ±9 of the right one.
FairnessApples spawn only on cells the head can reach. If the body boxes the head in, the pose resets without a penalty. Entering the tail's cell is legal unless that tick grows the snake.
LifecycleBackgrounding the app auto-pauses, and resuming takes an explicit tap. One frame loop. Best scores are per session in the prototype (DataStore in the app).

Historical layout specimens · portrait phone 390 × 844dpNative Home instead uses bounded no-scroll layout, responsive mascot/spacing, both mode scores, Settings only and the tighter title. Phone boards use 12 × 16 cells; landscape tablets use 20 × 16. All cells stay square.

Do / Don't

DOKeep one lime primary action per screen.
DON'TAdd arrow buttons or a D-pad. Controls are swipe only.
DOShow every score in gold number styles.
DON'THighlight the correct answer apple in any way.
DOKeep foliage at screen edges, behind content.
DON'TPut decorations on cells that hold the snake or an apple.
DOUse real × ÷ − glyphs in math.
DON'TIntroduce new hues. Stay within forest, lime, gold, cream and red.
