Snake & Math · UI Design v2

Home and MDAS gameplay screens for phone (portrait, 390×844 dp) and tablet (landscape, 1280×800 dp). All artwork is original and drawn in SVG. The mascot is a new character with a leaf sprout, round eyes, blush cheeks and dot spots. No reference artwork was traced or reused.

Nunito 700/800 for labels, captions and body copy. Fredoka handles titles, scores and math expressions, where rounded, chunky numerals help kids read them.

Mascot & game pieces

  • Signature details: a leaf sprout, round eyes with two glints, blush cheeks, and evenly spaced dot spots.
  • In-game, the body is one connected path through cell centres with rounded joins and a tapered tail.

Layout notes

  • Phone is portrait and stacks HUD, question, board and hint. Tablet is landscape, with the board on the left and the question and stats on the right.
  • Controls are swipe-only, with no D-pad or arrow buttons.
  • The correct apple is never styled differently from the others.
  • An empty heart shows a lost life, so the player can see what's left at a glance.
  • Touch targets are at least 46 dp. Cream on forest green passes WCAG AA.
